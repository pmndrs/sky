`@pmndrs/sky` is a port of Sébastien Hillaire's [_A Scalable and Production
Ready Sky and Atmosphere Rendering Technique_](https://sebh.github.io/publications/egsr2020.pdf)
(EGSR 2020) to Three.js TSL, running on the WebGPU renderer. It gives you a
physically-based sky that you drop into a scene as `scene.background` +
`scene.environment` (IBL) with one class, and scales up to aerial-perspective
haze on distant geometry and planet-scale (ground-to-orbit) rendering when
you need it.

## Why

Most Three.js sky solutions are either a static HDRI (no time-of-day, no
atmosphere response to camera altitude) or a cheap analytic gradient
(Preetham-style — looks flat, no aerial perspective). `@pmndrs/sky` instead
bakes the real multi-scattering atmosphere integral into a small set of
lookup textures (LUTs) once per sun/atmosphere change, so you get:

- A physically-plausible sky that responds correctly to time of day,
  latitude, and camera altitude — including views from space.
- Aerial-perspective haze: distant geometry desaturates and blues out with
  distance, the way real haze does, driven by the same LUTs as the sky
  background.
- Planet-scale support: fly from the ground up through the atmosphere to
  orbit without a "seam" between two different sky implementations.
- A tiny per-frame cost — the expensive multi-scattering integration is
  baked into LUTs and only re-runs when the sun or atmosphere parameters
  change; per-frame work is a handful of cheap LUT samples plus (optionally)
  a 32×32×32 aerial-perspective compute pass.

## What you get

- **`Sky`** — the 90% facade. Construct it, `attach()` it to a scene, call
  `update(camera)` every frame. See [Your First Sky](./your-first-sky).
- **`applyHaze`** — an opt-in post-process node for aerial-perspective haze
  on your scene's opaque geometry. See the [Haze guide](../guides/haze).
- **Planet-scale support** via `planetCenter` — fly from a few metres off
  the ground to orbit with one continuous atmosphere model. See the
  [Planet-scale guide](../guides/planet-scale).
- **`SkyAtmosphereBaker`** and the individual LUT classes for power users who
  want to swap pieces or drive the pipeline directly. See the
  [API reference](../api/baker).
- Curated atmosphere presets (`earth`, `mars`, `titan`) and full control over
  every Hillaire parameter. See [Tuning the atmosphere](../guides/tuning-atmosphere).

## Requirements

`@pmndrs/sky` targets the **WebGPU renderer** (`three/webgpu`,
`THREE.WebGPURenderer`) — see [Installation](./installation) for peer
dependency versions and WebGPU browser support notes.

## Status

Baked sky, aerial-perspective haze, and planet-scale (ground→orbit)
rendering are all functional today. Volumetric clouds and god-rays are out
of scope for this library.
