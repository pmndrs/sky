SkyView LUT, full integrator: WGSL vs TSL
Two SkyViewLUT instances (backend:'wgsl' / 'tsl') sharing one Transmittance + Multi-Scatter LUT, each rendered to a float target and diffed. This exercises the whole integrator in WGSL — medium sample, sun-transmittance (manual bilinear), earth shadow, MS-LUT feedback, power-series in-scatter, ground bounce. Values are tiny (per-unit-sun radiance, ~0.001–0.04), so relative diff is reported too. The canvas shows the WGSL SkyView LUT (×40).
